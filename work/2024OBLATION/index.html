OBLATION

Critical Interactive Installation on Digital Labour

OBLATION asks visitors to feed the installation tokens representing labour. The resulting audiovisual system becomes increasingly dazzling and disordered, revealing the hidden human cost behind digital prosperity.

Focus
Critical Design · Digital Labour · Interactive Installation
Medium
Tokens, sensing, kinetic mechanisms, light and sound
